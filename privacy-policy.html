Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Welcome to TinyShrink. Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains how TinyShrink handles information when you visit our website and use our online image compressor.

1. How Image Compression Works

TinyShrink is designed to process images directly in your web browser. When you use the image compression feature, the selected image is processed on your device and is not intentionally uploaded to a TinyShrink image-processing server.

Because processing happens in your browser, you can use the compressor without creating an account. Please remember that your browser, device, or other extensions may handle data according to their own settings and policies.

2. Information We Collect

TinyShrink does not ask you to create an account or provide personal information to use its basic image compression feature. We do not intentionally collect the images you select through the compressor.

However, the website is hosted using GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process technical information, such as IP addresses, browser details, timestamps, and server logs, as part of operating and securing its service. Such processing is subject to the provider's policies.

3. Cookies and Analytics

TinyShrink's basic image compression feature does not require an account or cookies to function. Hosting services, external resources, or tools added to the website in the future may use cookies or similar technologies.

If analytics tools are introduced, they may collect information about visits and interactions to help us understand website usage. Any such tools should be reflected in an updated version of this policy.

4. Advertising

TinyShrink may consider displaying advertisements in the future to support website maintenance. If advertising services are added, their providers may use cookies or similar technologies to deliver, measure, or personalize advertisements, depending on the service and your settings.

This section describes a possible future use of advertising. It does not mean that advertisements are currently active.

5. Third-Party Websites

TinyShrink may link to third-party websites or services. We do not control their content, privacy practices, or security. Please review the privacy policies of those services when you visit them.

6. Data Security

Processing images in your browser is intended to reduce the need to transmit your selected images to a processing server. However, no website or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Please use a trusted device and browser.

7. Children's Privacy

TinyShrink is a general-purpose image utility and is not specifically directed at children. If you believe that personal information relating to a child has been provided to us, please contact us through our project page.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when TinyShrink's features, hosting arrangements, analytics, or advertising practices change. The latest revision date will be shown at the top of this page.

9. Contact

For questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, you can contact the project maintainer through the TinyShrink GitHub Issues page .
