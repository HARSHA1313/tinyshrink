Frequently Asked Questions

1. Is TinyShrink free to use?

Yes, TinyShrink is a free online image compression tool. You can compress supported images without creating an account.

2. Are my images uploaded to a server?

TinyShrink processes images directly in your browser. Your images are not intended to be uploaded to a compression server.

3. Which image formats does TinyShrink support?

TinyShrink supports JPG, PNG and WebP images. Available output formats depend on the options provided by the tool.

4. Can I use TinyShrink on my phone?

Yes, you can use TinyShrink in a modern mobile browser on a compatible phone.

5. Will image compression reduce quality?

It can, depending on the format and compression settings. Preview your compressed image before downloading it.

6. Do I need to install software?

No installation is required. Open TinyShrink in your browser and select an image to get started.
